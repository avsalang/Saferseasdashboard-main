# MARINA terminology and required reporting information

**Goal:** Use recognizable source terms and require information consumed by MARINA reports, matrices, maps and figures at the relevant workflow stage.

**User direction:** “make sure field names are standard (MARINA or IMO) ... Make the fields required if they are needed in the MARINA reports, figures, or tables”. Preserve the approved existing SAFERSEAS interface, seven sections, account roles and digital assistance.

**Decision:** Prefer supplied MARINA labels; explain IMO equivalence rather than silently recoding local classifications. A required factual field must have a value or an explicit field-specific Unknown/Not Applicable explanation. Never accept an untouched default unknown as complete or invent a zero/position/identifier/cause. Drafts remain saveable. Initial submission, validation and report issuance have different completeness requirements. Outputs with unresolved requirements are identified as working copies. Source form presence is evidence of an information need, not a claim of legal mandatory status.

**Architecture:** Shared pure requirement assessment and stable exception keys; contextual completeness panel; requirements enforced by the local store and report lifecycle. Keep explanations inside incident/report snapshots and export appendices. Use source-based labels without renaming stable data keys or original spreadsheet headers.
